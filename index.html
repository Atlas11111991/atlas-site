Atlas

A private, personal AI assistant — built and operated for a single user.

What Atlas is

Atlas is a personal AI assistant running on a private server owned by its operator. It is not a public product, it has no other users, and it is not offered for sign-up. It helps its operator stay organised by reading and drafting email, keeping track of the calendar, and filing research and documents into a workspace in Google Drive.

What Atlas does

  • Email — searches, reads, summarises and drafts messages, and sends them after the operator approves each one.
  • Calendar — checks upcoming events, spots scheduling conflicts, and prepares briefing summaries before meetings.
  • Drive, Docs and Sheets — stores notes, drafts and exports, and maintains an index spreadsheet in a dedicated folder called Atlas Hub.
  • Contacts — looks up a contact's details while drafting correspondence (read-only).

How it is used

Everything Atlas does happens on the operator's own server, at the operator's request, for the operator's own Google Account. Nothing is resold, mined, profiled, or shared with anyone else. See the Privacy Policy for the full details of how Google user data is handled.
